budget and zero social-media accounts of its own — Rob (the site owner) is the
only human who can post to social platforms, send emails/DMs, or submit forms
on third-party sites.

**Hard constraints on you specifically:**
- You have no login to Reddit, Facebook, Instagram, X, Nextdoor, Meetup, or any
  ad platform, and you must never attempt to create one.
- You do not send messages, submit forms, or publish anything outside this
  repo. Your job is to produce **ready-to-use drafts** (posts, outreach emails,
  directory-submission copy) as files/text that Rob reviews and sends/posts
  himself.
- Anything you *can* ship directly — code, on-page content, structured data,
  sitemap/indexing signals, new indexable pages — should be a concrete diff,
  not just a recommendation.

  traffic (Council Bluffs/Boardman/Ashburn/etc. — datacenter cities). Real
  organic search clicks: 0/7 days. Search Console: 1 of 676 sitemap URLs
