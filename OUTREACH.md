## Facebook group post drafts

### Rhody Pickleballers
> Hey all — longtime lurker, built something I think this group might find useful.
>
> I kept losing track of which RI clubs have open play on which nights, so I put together Open Play RI (openplayri.com) — a free, no-login directory of pickleball venues across the state, plus live schedules (real times/open spots, pulled straight from each club's own booking system) for six clubs so far: the JCC of Greater RI, Pickleball Citi, Ocean State Pickleball, East Bay Pickleball Club, LIL Rhody, and Centerline. Everything else on the site (36 venues across 21 towns right now) is a directory listing with address/hours/map.
>
> You can filter by town, sort by distance ("near me"), and subscribe to a calendar feed (.ics) so sessions show up on your phone automatically. No ads, no account needed — I just wanted this to exist.
>
> If you spot a wrong address, a missing club, or a stale schedule, tell me here and I'll fix it. Also open to adding more live schedules if your club uses something scrapable (CourtReserve, a public calendar, etc.) — suggestions welcome.

### RI Pickleball Tour
> Hi everyone — sharing a free tool in case it's useful between tournaments.
>
> I built Open Play RI (openplayri.com), a statewide directory + live schedule tracker for pickleball open play/drop-in sessions. Right now it has live, auto-updating schedules for six clubs (JCC of Greater RI, Pickleball Citi, Ocean State Pickleball, East Bay Pickleball Club, LIL Rhody, Centerline) plus a directory of ~30 more places to play with addresses/hours — sortable by town or by distance from you.
